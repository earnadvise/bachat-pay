What is BachatPay?

BachatPay is a mobile-first USDC payments app built on Arc — Circle's blockchain where USDC is the native gas token. It works like GPay or PhonePe, but instead of a bank account, users connect a crypto wallet and transact in USDC with near-zero fees and sub-second finality.

It is designed for everyday Indian users — merchants, households, and savings groups — who want the benefits of stablecoins without the complexity of DeFi.

The Problem

India has 500M+ UPI users but zero financial sovereignty. Every transaction goes through a bank, a payment processor, and a regulator. Accounts can be frozen, transfers blocked, and fees extracted at every step.

Crypto alternatives exist but are too complex, too volatile, or too expensive. There is no simple, stable, low-cost onchain payment app designed for Indian users.

Key Features
💸
Instant USDC Payments
Send to any wallet via QR scan or address. Sub-second on Arc. Gas under $0.001.
🏪
Merchant QR Card
Shopkeepers generate a branded QR with shop name. Download PNG or print. No bank needed.
🐷
Bachat Vault
Lock USDC toward a savings goal with a target date. 2% ragequit penalty enforced onchain.
👥
Chit Fund (ROSCA)
Onchain rotating savings group. 2–20 members. Transparent, trustless, automatic rotation.
₹
Live INR Conversion
Real-time USDC to INR rate. Enter amounts in rupees. See balance in INR instantly.
🌉
Auto Bridge
Bridge USDC from Base, Arbitrum, Optimism or Polygon to Arc in one tap via Circle CCTP.
